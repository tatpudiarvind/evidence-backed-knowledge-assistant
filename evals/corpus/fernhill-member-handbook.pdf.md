# Fernhill Makerspace Member Handbook

This handbook is synthetic. Fernhill Makerspace, its address, its fees, and its rules were invented to test a document question-answering tool. Any resemblance to a real organization is coincidental.

Fernhill Makerspace is a members' workshop at 14 Quarry Lane. Members share tools for woodworking, electronics, sewing, and digital fabrication, and they help to run the space. This handbook covers membership, opening hours, inductions, machine bookings, storage, payments, and conduct. The rules for individual machines are in the separate Equipment Safety Policy.

# 1. Membership tiers and fees

Fernhill offers three membership tiers. Fees are charged monthly.

- Standard membership costs EUR 35 per month. Standard members may use the workshop whenever it is staffed.
- Keyholder membership costs EUR 55 per month and comes with a personal door fob. To become a keyholder, a member needs at least three months of standard membership and must complete the keyholder induction.
- Concession membership costs EUR 18 per month. It is open to students, people over 65, and people receiving unemployment support. Concession members have the same access as standard members.

New members pay a one-off joining fee of EUR 20, which covers the general induction. The joining fee is waived for concession members.

To join, fill in the membership form at the front desk while the workshop is staffed. Concession members show proof of eligibility when they join; the front desk does not keep a copy of it.

---

# 2. Opening hours

The workshop is staffed from 16:00 to 22:00 from Tuesday to Friday, and from 10:00 to 18:00 on Saturdays and Sundays. A trained host is on site whenever the workshop is staffed. The makerspace is closed on Mondays for cleaning and maintenance.

Keyholders may use the workshop at any time outside staffed hours, including on Mondays, as long as they follow the lone working rules for high-risk machines in the Equipment Safety Policy.

The makerspace closes for a winter break from 24 December to 1 January. Door fobs do not work during the winter break.

# 3. Guests and visitors

Members may bring up to two guests at a time while the workshop is staffed. Every guest signs the visitor book at the front desk, stays with the member who invited them, and may not use powered tools. A guest may visit up to three times a year; after that, they are asked to join.

# 4. Children and young people

Children under 12 may only take part in the monthly family workshop. Young people aged 12 to 15 may use hand tools if a parent or guardian stays with them the whole time. People aged 16 or 17 may become members with written consent from a parent or guardian.

---

# 5. Inductions

Every new member attends a general induction before using the workshop. General inductions run every Wednesday at 18:30 and every Saturday at 11:00, and each one takes about 90 minutes. It covers the fire exits, the first aid kit, dust extraction, and how to book machines. Wear closed shoes and bring your membership form.

Machines with a yellow label also need a machine induction, which the machine's tool steward gives in small groups. A machine induction is valid for 12 months. If you have not used a machine for 12 months, ask its tool steward for a short refresher before using it again.

# 6. Booking machines

The laser cutter, the CNC router, and the 3D printers are booked in advance on the booking board by the front desk. Each member may book at most four hours per week on each of these machines. If a booked session has not started within 15 minutes of its start time, the booking is released to other members.

A member who misses three bookings in a row may not book any machine for two weeks. Cancel a booking you cannot use by crossing it out on the board.

---

# 7. Storage

Lockers can be rented for EUR 6 per month from the front desk, which also issues the keys.

Work in progress can stay on the project shelves for up to 14 days. Every item on the shelves needs a project tag showing the member's name and the date. Untagged items are moved to lost property after seven days, and lost property is cleared out after a further 21 days.

Hazardous liquids, such as solvents and resin, are never stored in lockers or on the shelves. They go in the yellow flammables cabinet.

# 8. The members' shop

The members' shop sells plywood, acrylic sheet, filament, fasteners, and electronic parts at cost plus 10 percent. The shop is card only and does not accept cash. Offcuts in the scrap bins are free for every member.

# 9. Cleaning up

Members clean their work area and hang tools back on the shadow boards before leaving. Dust extraction stays on for five minutes after the last cut. The last member to leave switches off the main power at the red panel next to the kitchen and sets the alarm.

---

# 10. Payments and cancellation

Fees are paid monthly by direct debit on the first working day of the month. To cancel, give 30 days' notice through the noticeboard form. Fees already paid for part of a month are not refunded.

Members can pause their membership for up to eight weeks a year, for example while traveling or ill. No fees are charged during a pause, and door fobs stay switched off until it ends.

# 11. Code of conduct

Fernhill is a shared space. Members treat each other with respect, ask before using anyone else's project or materials, and leave tools in better condition than they found them. Harassment of any kind leads to suspension while the board reviews the complaint.

# 12. Faults and questions

If a tool is damaged or unsafe, stop using it, hang a red fault tag on it, and tell the host on duty. Only the machine's tool steward repairs it.

Questions about membership go to the front desk while the workshop is staffed, or through the noticeboard form.
