# Fernhill Makerspace Equipment Safety Policy

This policy is synthetic. It was written to test a document question-answering tool. Its rules are not safety advice for any real workshop or machine.

All members follow this policy. Tool stewards may add rules for their own machine; those rules are posted next to the machine.

# Protective equipment and age limits

The table shows the protective equipment, the minimum age, and whether a machine induction is needed for each machine.

| Machine | Protective equipment | Minimum age | Machine induction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Laser cutter | None | 16 and over | Required |
| CNC router | Safety glasses and ear defenders | 18 and over | Required |
| Wood lathe | Face shield; no gloves | 18 and over | Required |
| Metal bandsaw | Safety glasses | 18 and over | Required |
| 3D printers | None | 12 and over | Required |
| Soldering stations | Safety glasses; fume extractor switched on | 12 and over | Not required |
| Sewing machines | None | 12 and over | Not required |

# Laser cutter

Stay within reach of the laser cutter for as long as it is cutting. Never cut PVC, vinyl, or any other material that contains chlorine: it releases a corrosive, toxic gas. Check the approved material list next to the machine before cutting anything new.

If a flame does not go out by itself within three seconds, press the red stop button, keep the lid closed, and use the CO2 extinguisher mounted beside the machine. Report the fire as an incident afterward.

# Wood lathe

Never wear gloves at the wood lathe, because they can be pulled into the spinning work. Tie back long hair, roll up loose sleeves, and take off jewelry before switching on. Check that the work is held firmly and that the tool rest is locked.

# 3D printers

A 3D printer may run unattended for up to eight hours, as long as the member who started the print stays in the building. Only PLA and PETG filament may be printed. ABS is not allowed because of its fumes.

# High-risk machines and lone working

The CNC router, the wood lathe, and the metal bandsaw are high-risk machines. A high-risk machine may only be used when at least two inducted people are in the workshop. The rule also applies when the workshop is not staffed.

# Reporting incidents

Record every accident, near miss, or damaged guard in the incident book at the front desk on the same day. If anyone needs hospital treatment, the host on duty tells the board chair within 24 hours. The first aid kit and the defibrillator are by the main entrance.

# Fire safety

The fire exits are the front doors and the back door beside the loading bay. The assembly point is the car park opposite the main entrance, next to the blue container. Fire drills are held every April and October.
