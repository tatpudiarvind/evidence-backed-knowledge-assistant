Fernhill Makerspace - Volunteer FAQ

This FAQ is synthetic. It was written to test a document question-answering tool and describes a makerspace that does not exist.

Q: How do I start volunteering?
A: Come to the volunteer welcome evening on the first Thursday of every month at 19:00 in the classroom. You will meet the volunteer coordinator and choose a role.

Q: What roles are there?
A: Most volunteers start as front desk hosts. Others help at weekend events, sort donated materials, or look after the website.

Q: How long is a front desk shift?
A: A front desk shift lasts three hours. Hosts welcome visitors, check guests in, and answer questions.

Q: Is there a minimum commitment?
A: No. Volunteer as often or as rarely as suits you.

Q: Do volunteers get anything in return?
A: Every 12 hours of volunteering earns one free month of standard membership. The volunteer coordinator keeps a record of your hours.

Q: How do I become a tool steward?
A: You need at least six months of membership, a recommendation from a current tool steward, and a short teaching assessment. Tool stewards give machine inductions and look after their machine.

Q: What if I can't make my shift?
A: Tell the volunteer coordinator no later than two days before the shift, so that someone else can cover it.

Q: Is there any training?
A: Yes. New hosts shadow an experienced host for their first two shifts, and every host takes a first aid refresher once a year.

Q: Who coordinates the volunteers?
A: The volunteer coordinator, who is elected every year at the annual general meeting (AGM) in May.

Q: Can I work on my own projects during a shift?
A: Yes, during quiet periods, as long as the front desk is never left empty.
