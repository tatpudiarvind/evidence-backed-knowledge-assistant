# Quarry Lane Community Garden - Autumn Newsletter

This newsletter is synthetic. The garden, its plots, and its events were invented to test a document question-answering tool.

Welcome to the autumn newsletter of the Quarry Lane Community Garden, the shared garden at the far end of Quarry Lane. The garden is run by a volunteer committee and is separate from the makerspace down the road.

# Plots for next season

Garden plots are allocated by lottery every January. A plot costs EUR 40 per year, and each household may hold one plot. Plots that are not cultivated by June are offered to the next household on the waiting list.

# Opening times

The garden gates open at 08:00 and close at dusk every day of the year. The tool shed is open whenever a committee member is in the garden.
