Q: When does the swim school run?

Q: How long is a lesson?
A: Every lesson lasts 40 minutes.

Q: How many children are in a class?
A: At most six in the beginner classes, and at most ten in the others.

Q: How much do lessons cost?
A: A term costs EUR 72 for children and EUR 96 for adults, paid when you book.

Q: How do we book, or book again for the next term?
A: Book online. Pupils who are already in the swim school can rebook during the last two weeks of their term, before booking opens to everyone else.

Q: What happens if my child misses a lesson?
A: Missed lessons are not refunded, but every pupil may book one make-up lesson per term at reception.

Q: Can parents watch?
A: Yes, from the viewing terrace above the cafe. Parents stay off the poolside during lessons.

Q: What should my child bring?
A: A swimsuit, a towel, and goggles if they want them. Floats are provided, and armbands are not used in lessons.

Q: How do children move up?
A: Teachers assess every child in the final week of term. There are seven levels, from Tadpoles to Dolphins, and every child who passes a level gets a badge and a certificate.

Q: Who are the teachers?
A: Every teacher holds a national swimming teaching qualification and has passed a background check.
