# Wrenfield Lido Swimmer Guide

This guide is synthetic. Wrenfield Lido, its prices, and its rules were invented to test a document question-answering tool. Any resemblance to a real pool is coincidental.

Wrenfield Lido is an open-air swimming pool in Wrenfield Park, run by the Wrenfield Lido Trust, a small charity. Next to the main pool are a paddling pool for young children, a lawn, and a cafe. This guide covers the season and opening times, prices, booking, lane swimming, children, the cold water club, lockers, the cafe, accessibility, refunds, conduct, and the Friends program. The lifeguards' rules are in the separate Pool Safety Procedures.

# 1. Season and opening times

The summer season runs from 1 May to 30 September. During the season the main pool is heated to 24 degrees Celsius, and each morning's water temperature is written on the board at the entrance.

- Early swim, for lane swimming only, runs from 06:30 to 09:00 on weekdays.
- General swim runs from 10:00 to 19:00 every day.
- On Thursdays the pool stays open for a late swim until 21:30.

On the first Monday of every month the lido opens at 13:00, after the pool has been deep cleaned.

---

# 2. Prices

A single swim costs:

- EUR 6 for adults;
- EUR 3 for children aged 4 to 15, while children under 4 swim free with a paying adult;
- EUR 4 for students, people over 65, and people receiving disability benefits.

A family ticket costs EUR 16 and covers two adults and up to three children. A 10-swim card costs EUR 50 and is valid for 12 months from the day it is bought. Season tickets cost EUR 140 for adults and EUR 70 for children, and cover every session of the summer season.

# 3. Booking

Swims are booked online, up to seven days ahead. A quarter of the places in every general swim are kept for people who turn up without booking. General swim is divided into sessions of 90 minutes. Arrive 10 minutes before your session starts: swimmers who arrive more than 20 minutes late may be turned away when the lido is busy.

---

# 4. Lane swimming

During general swim, three lanes are kept for lane swimming, marked slow, medium, and fast. Choose the lane that matches your speed and swim counterclockwise. Overtake only at the end of the lane, never in the middle, and rest in the corner so that others can turn. Fins and hand paddles may only be used in the fast lane.

# 5. Children

Children under 8 must be in the water with an adult aged 16 or over, and each adult may bring at most two children under 8. Children aged 8 to 11 may swim without an adult in the water, as long as the adult who brought them stays at the lido. Young people aged 12 and over may come on their own. Children may only swim in the deep end if they wear the right wristband; the lifeguards explain the colors.

# 6. The cold water club

The pool is not heated from October to April, when it is open only to the Wrenfield Cold Water Club. Club members swim on Saturday and Sunday mornings from 08:00 to 09:30. To join, complete a cold water induction with a lifeguard and pay EUR 30 for the winter. When the water is colder than 10 degrees, swims last no more than 15 minutes.

---

# 7. Changing rooms and lockers

The changing village has private cubicles and two family changing rooms. Lockers take a EUR 1 coin, which you get back when you unlock them. Lockers are emptied at closing time, and anything left behind goes to lost property, where it is kept for 30 days. Valuables can be handed in at reception in a sealed bag.

# 8. The cafe

The Lido Cafe is open from 11:00 to 17:00 on every day the pool is open. Glass is not allowed on the poolside or the lawn. Picnics are welcome on the lawn, but barbecues and disposable grills are not allowed anywhere in the lido.

# 9. Accessibility

There is step-free access from the park entrance on Mill Race Road. The main pool has a hoist at the shallow end, and the paddling pool has a ramp. A caregiver who comes with a disabled swimmer is admitted without charge. On Tuesdays from 19:30 to 20:30 there is a quiet session for anyone who finds the lido too busy or loud, with no music and no whistles except in an emergency.

---

# 10. Refunds and cancellations

If the lido cancels a session, for example because of a thunderstorm, the booking is refunded to your card within five working days. You can cancel your own booking up to 24 hours before it starts; the money is then kept as credit, which you can use until the end of the season. Later cancellations, and swims that are booked but not used, are not refunded.

# 11. Conduct

No running, pushing, or dive-bombing. Photos and videos are never allowed in the changing village; on the poolside, photograph or film only your own group. Inflatables may only be used in the inflatable session on Saturday afternoons. Anyone who ignores a lifeguard is asked to leave, and may be banned for the rest of the season.

# 12. Friends of Wrenfield Lido

Friends of Wrenfield Lido is the trust's supporters' program, and costs EUR 25 a year. Friends can book swims nine days ahead instead of seven, get 10 percent off at the cafe, and can vote at the trust's annual meeting in October.
