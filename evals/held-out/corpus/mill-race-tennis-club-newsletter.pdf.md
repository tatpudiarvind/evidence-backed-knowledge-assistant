# Mill Race Tennis Club - Summer Newsletter

This newsletter is synthetic. The club, its courts, and its events were invented to test a document question-answering tool.

Welcome to the summer newsletter of the Mill Race Tennis Club, whose six courts are next to the lido in Wrenfield Park. The club is run by its members and is not part of Wrenfield Lido.

# Courts and membership

Courts can be booked from 07:00 to 21:00, with floodlights after dark. Adult membership costs EUR 180 a year, and junior membership EUR 45 a year. Visitors pay EUR 12 per court per hour.

---

# Summer tournament

The summer tournament takes place on Sunday 12 July 2026, starting at 10:30. Singles and doubles are open to members and visitors, and entry costs EUR 8 per player. Prizes are given out at 18:30.

# Junior coaching

Junior coaching for players aged 7 to 16 runs on Wednesday evenings during school terms. New juniors can try two sessions before they join.
