# Wrenfield Lido Pool Safety Procedures

This document is synthetic. It was written to test a document question-answering tool. Its procedures are not safety advice for any real pool.

All staff and volunteers follow these procedures. The duty manager may close the pool, or any part of it, whenever it is unsafe. The general manager reviews the procedures every March.

# Lifeguards and bather limits

The table shows how many lifeguards must be on duty, and the most bathers allowed in the water at once, for each kind of session.

| Session | Lifeguards on duty | Most bathers in the water |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General swim | 5 | 280 |
| Early swim | 2 | 60 |
| Inflatable session | 6 | 150 |
| Swim school | 1, as well as the teachers | 48 |
| Quiet session | 2 | 35 |
| Cold water club | 2 | 25 |

# Wristbands

Children under 12 wear a wristband that shows where they may swim. A lifeguard gives it out after a short swim test at the shallow end.

| Wristband | Who wears it | Where they may swim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | Children who swam 25 meters and trod water for one minute | The whole pool, including the deep end |
| Yellow | Children who swam 10 meters | The shallow end, up to the floating rope |
| Red | Children who have not passed the test | Only within reach of their adult |

# Depth and diving

The main pool is 0.9 meters deep at the shallow end and 2.2 meters deep at the deep end. Diving is allowed only from the deep end wall, never from the sides, and only while a lifeguard is in the tall chair. There is no diving board.

# Clearing the pool

To clear the pool, a lifeguard blows three long blasts on the whistle. Swimmers leave the water by the nearest steps and wait on the lawn until a lifeguard says it is safe to go back in. If the lido has to be evacuated, everyone leaves by the nearest exit and gathers at the bandstand in the park.

# Thunder and lightning

If a lifeguard hears thunder or sees lightning, the pool is cleared at once. It stays closed until 30 minutes after the last thunder or lightning. Swimmers wait in the changing village or the cafe, never under the trees.

# Water quality

The pool water is tested every two hours while the lido is open, and each result is written in the plant room log. If a test fails, the duty manager closes the pool until a new test passes.

# Chemicals and the plant room

Pool chemicals are kept in the locked plant room next to the boiler house. Only staff who hold the plant room certificate may go in. Chemicals are delivered on Wednesday mornings, before the lido opens, and two members of staff must be there to receive them.

# Incidents and first aid

Every injury, rescue, or near miss is recorded in the online incident log before the end of the shift. The defibrillator and the first aid room are next to the lifeguard hut. If an ambulance is called, a member of staff waits at the park gate to guide it in.
